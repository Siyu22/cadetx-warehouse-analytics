# Draft KPIs & Project Hypotheses
## Core KPIs
1. Inventory turnover = total COGS / average inventory value
2. Days inventory outstanding = 365 / inventory turnover
3. Supplier on-time delivery %
4. Warehouse throughput (units per month)
5. Slow-moving stock % (no demand in > 90 days)
6. Dead stock % (no demand > 180 days)
7. Stockout rate
8. Average order value
9. Customer retention rate
10. Space utilisation %

## Hypotheses
H1: A small subset of SKUs holds most inventory value (Pareto/ABC)
H2: Some warehouses have consistently poorer space utilisation and throughput
H3: Supplier reliability varies and impacts stock availability
H4: Many slow-moving products are overstocked against actual demand
H5: Customer buying behaviour can be segmented with RFM for targeted planning
